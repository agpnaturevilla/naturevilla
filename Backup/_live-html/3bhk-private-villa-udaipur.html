The 3-Bedroom Private Estate: Detailed Amenities & Layout

A technical deep-dive into Udaipur's most exclusive 3BHK nature retreat.

While our homepage gives you the overview, this page details exactly what you get when you book the "Private Kingdom." From the filtration technology of the Blue Wave pool to the equipment list in the Little Paris kitchen, here is everything you need to know.

The "Blue Wave" Pool: Technical Specifications

Unlike shared resort pools, the Blue Wave is engineered for privacy and hygiene. It is not just a water body; it is a curated zone for your group.

  • Dimensions: 242 Sq. Ft. of swimming area.
  • Depth Profile: Designed for families, with safe zones for children and ample depth for adults to swim laps.
  • Hygiene Protocol: We use a high-grade filtration system that runs daily. The water is crystal clear, pH-balanced, and chemically treated to international safety standards—ensuring no skin irritation for kids.
  • Night Mode: The pool features underwater LED ambient lighting, turning the deck into a glowing lounge space for your evening cocktails.

Dual-Kitchen Architecture: "Laziz" & "Little Paris"

We are one of the few villas in Udaipur offering two distinct culinary zones, perfect for long-stay groups who crave home-cooked meals.

Zone A: Laziz (The Indoor Command Center)

Located centrally to serve all three bedrooms, this modern kitchen is fully stocked for functional cooking.

  • Appliances: Double-door refrigerator, microwave oven, gas hob, and mixer-grinder.
  • Cookware: Full sets of pans, pressure cookers, and serving bowls sufficient for 15 guests.
  • Essentials Provided: RO water purifier, tea/coffee making supplies, and basic spices.

Zone B: The Little Paris (Outdoor BBQ Deck)

Designed for the "Experience," this open-air kitchenette is where memories are made.

  • The Vibe: Chic, rustic brickwork open to the sky and mountain views.
  • Equipment: Professional-grade coal barbecue grill (Coal provided on request).
  • Usage: Perfect for winter bonfires, grilling paneer tikkas, or enjoying a sundowner while the kids play in the garden.

The Sleeping Quarters: 3 Distinct "Micro-Environments"

AGP Nature Villa is a 3BHK configuration designed to offer privacy even within your group. Each room has a distinct personality.

  • Bedroom 1: The Love Nest. A cozy, warm-toned sanctuary designed for couples. Features soft lighting, blackout curtains for sleeping in, and an en-suite bathroom with premium fittings.
  • Bedroom 2: Mountain Peak. The room with a view. Large glass windows frame the jagged Aravali peaks, allowing you to wake up to the sunrise without leaving your bed.
  • Bedroom 3: Valley View. The most spacious room, overlooking the Infinity Garden and the valley below. Ideal for families with young children who need extra floor space.

Capacity Note: While we have 3 bedrooms, we comfortably host groups of up to 15 using high-quality extra bedding and convertible spaces.

What Does a Day at AGP Nature Villa Look Like?

To help you visualize your stay, here is a typical itinerary our guests enjoy:

  • 07:00 AM: Yoga on the Infinity Garden lawn while the peacocks call from the hills.
  • 09:00 AM: Breakfast by the pool. Our helper sets up the table while you enjoy a morning dip in the Blue Wave.
  • 11:00 AM: A short 10-minute drive to Badi Lake for a peaceful walk, or a trek up to Bahubali Hills for the best selfie in Udaipur.
  • 01:00 PM: Lunch delivered by local partners (authentic Dal Baati Churma) or cooked fresh in Laziz.
  • 04:00 PM: Tea time at Heat Heaven. As the sun dips behind the Aravalis, the bonfire is prepped.
  • 08:00 PM: BBQ night at Little Paris. Music playing, coals glowing, and the entire property lit up just for you.
